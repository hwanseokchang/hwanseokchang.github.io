About

My research aims to create functional 3D nanoarchitectures that overcome intrinsic material limitations through geometry. I focus on discovering structures with tailored performance for sensing, structural, and photonic applications.

My goal is to connect desired performance with architectures that can be manufactured at scale and converted into functional materials while preserving their geometry. This would bring architected metamaterials closer to practical use across different material systems.

Research Interests

Inverse design and AI-assisted optimization of 3D nanoarchitectures
Scalable 3D nanofabrication
Architected materials and metamaterials for sensing, structural, photonic applications
Material conversion to functional materials
